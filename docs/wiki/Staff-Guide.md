# Staff Guide

This guide covers everything a staff member needs to use Shiftable day-to-day.

---

## Getting started

### Claiming your account

Your manager will send you a link — either by email or directly. Open it on your phone.

The link takes you to a setup screen where you choose a 4–6 digit PIN. This PIN is how you log in every day. Choose something you'll remember but others won't guess.

Once you set your PIN, the app will prompt you to add it to your home screen. Do this — it makes Shiftable feel and behave like a regular app, and enables push notifications.

### Logging in

Open Shiftable and enter your PIN on the keypad. If someone else at your restaurant happens to use the same PIN, you'll see a name picker — just tap your name.

### If you forget your PIN

Tap **Forgot PIN** on the login screen and enter your email address. You'll receive a reset link that works the same as the original claim link.

## Your schedule

### Dashboard

Your dashboard shows your next upcoming shift at the top. Below that are quick links to all features.

The bell icon in the top right shows your unread notification count.

### My Week

Shows all your shifts for the current week, with shift times and role. Tap the arrows to navigate to other weeks.

### Full Week Grid

Shows the full schedule for all staff. Useful for seeing who you're working with. You can filter by group using the buttons at the top.

On mobile, scroll horizontally to see the full week.

## Shift swaps

### Offering a swap

If you need someone to cover your shift:

1. Go to **Swaps** from the dashboard
2. Tap **Offer a Swap** and select the shift you want to give up
3. The swap is posted — eligible coworkers will see it and can claim it

You'll be notified when someone claims your swap. Depending on the situation, it may be approved automatically or go to your manager for review.

### Claiming a swap

When a coworker posts a swap you're eligible for, it appears in your Swaps list. Tap **Claim** to take it.

Auto-approval happens when: you're in the same group, you're available that day, and taking the shift won't push you over your weekly hour limit. If any of those conditions aren't met, it goes to the manager for review.

### Cancelling a swap you offered

If nobody has claimed your swap yet, you can cancel it. Go to **Swaps** and tap **Cancel** on your open swap.

## Time-off requests

Go to **Time Off** from the dashboard to request days off.

1. Tap **Request Time Off**
2. Select the start and end date of your request
3. Add a reason (optional but helpful for your manager)
4. Submit

Your request will show as **Pending** until your manager approves or denies it. You'll receive a notification when there's an update. If denied, your manager may add a note explaining why.

---

## Availability

Availability tells the scheduler which days and times you're available to work. It's used when building new schedules.

### Viewing your current availability

Go to **Availability** from the dashboard. Your current approved pattern is shown.

### Requesting a change

If your availability changes (new class, second job, etc.):

1. Go to **Availability** and tap **Request Change**
2. Set your available days and times
3. Choose an **Effective From** date — this must be a Monday, at least one week away
4. Submit

Your manager reviews the request. If approved, the new pattern takes effect from the date you chose. The scheduler will use it for all weeks from that point forward.

> Changes can't apply to a week that's already been published. Always submit availability changes as early as possible.

---

## Notifications

Go to **Notifications** to see all your recent alerts. Unread notifications are highlighted. Tap **Mark All Read** to clear the badge.

You'll receive notifications for:
- Schedule published for your week
- Someone claimed your swap offer
- Your swap was approved or denied
- Your time-off request was approved or denied
- Your availability change was approved or denied
- Announcements from your manager

### Enabling push notifications

Push notifications are sent to your device even when the app isn't open. To enable them, open the app and allow notifications when prompted.

If you missed the prompt, you can re-enable notifications through your device's app settings (look for Shiftable in your browser's site permissions, or in iOS Settings → Safari → Advanced → Website Data).
