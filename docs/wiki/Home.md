# Shiftable Documentation

Shiftable is a self-hosted, mobile-first restaurant scheduling PWA. It runs on your own server — no subscription, no third-party access to your staff data.

## What it does

- **Shift scheduling** — build and publish weekly schedules, with an auto-scheduler that fills shifts based on availability and coverage rules
- **Shift swaps** — staff request swaps, the system auto-approves safe ones and escalates the rest
- **Time-off requests** — staff submit requests, managers approve with optional notes
- **Availability management** — staff set their weekly availability patterns, effective from a future week
- **Push notifications** — real-time alerts for schedule changes, swap approvals, and announcements
- **Broadcast messaging** — send announcements to all staff or a specific group
- **Mobile PWA** — installs to the home screen on iOS and Android, works like a native app

## Who it's for

Restaurants and hospitality businesses with 1–60 staff who want a self-hosted alternative to scheduling SaaS products. You own the server, you own the data.
